
# Publicity

## Promotion Plan

- Create posters to promote the Campus Connect event.
- Share event information on Instagram.
- Put posters around the campus.
- Share event details in student group chats.
- Encourage students to invite their friends.

## Promotion Timeline

- Two weeks before: Design posters and prepare social media posts.
- One week before: Share posters on Instagram and student group chats.
- Three days before: Send reminders to students.
- Event day: Share event updates and photos.

## Publicity Materials

- Design posters with the event date, time and venue.
- Create social media posts to attract students.
- Prepare an online registration form.

## Questions for Discussion

- When should we start promoting the event?
- Which social media platform should we use?
- How many students are expected to join?
- Who will be responsible for designing the posters?
