
# Publicity

## Promotion Plan

- Create posters for the Campus Connect event.
- Share event information on Instagram.
- Put posters around the campus.
- Share event details in student group chats.
- Encourage students to invite their friends.

## Publicity Materials

- Design posters with the event date, time and venue.
- Create social media posts before the event.
- Prepare an online registration form.

## Questions for Discussion

- When should we start promoting the event?
- How many students are expected to join?
